About

I am a Lecturer at Shantou University. I completed my Ph.D. studies at the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences, in 2024, supervised by Dingkang Wang.

My research focuses on computer algebra and fully homomorphic encryption. I study polynomial matrices, Gröbner bases, and parametric polynomial systems, as well as algorithms for efficient computation on encrypted data.

Research Interests

Computer Algebra

Polynomial matrix factorizations and Smith forms; Gröbner and standard bases; parametric polynomial systems and rational univariate representations.

Homomorphic Encryption

Homomorphic matrix multiplication; functional bootstrapping; CKKS bootstrapping and efficient linear transformations.
